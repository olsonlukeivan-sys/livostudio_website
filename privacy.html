Privacy Policy

Last updated August 2026

Draw Closer is made by Livo Studio LLC. This policy explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

What we collect

Draw Closer does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no login, and no connection to any server.

Local storage

Draw Closer stores a small amount of data on your device to remember which cards you have seen and whether you have completed onboarding. This data never leaves your device.

Notifications

If you choose to enable daily reminders, Draw Closer schedules a local notification on your device. This is handled entirely by your phone's operating system. No notification data is sent to any server.

Third parties

Draw Closer does not use any advertising networks, analytics tools, or third-party data services.

Children

Draw Closer does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. There is nothing to collect.

Changes

If this policy changes in a future version of the app, we will update the date at the top of this page.
